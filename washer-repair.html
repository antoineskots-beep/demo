Frequently asked questions

Why is my washer not draining?

A clogged drain pump, a blocked or kinked drain hose and a dirty filter are the usual causes. If water is sitting in the drum, stop the machine and call us.

Why is my washer leaking?

Worn door seals, loose or cracked hoses and a failing pump are the most common sources. Turn off the water supply to the machine and call us.

Do you repair front-load and top-load washers of all brands?
