Expert appliance repair at your door

We repair washers, dryers, ovens, cooktops, refrigerators and dishwashers of all brands in Laval and Montreal. Pick an appliance to see what we fix.

Washer repair

Front-load and top-load washers, fixed in your home.

  • Won't drain or won't fill
  • Won't spin or stops mid-cycle
  • Leaks from the door or hoses
  • Loud banging or grinding
See all washer repair problems

Dryer repair

Dryers that run but leave clothes wet, or stop running at all.

  • Runs but doesn't heat
  • Drum won't turn
  • Takes several cycles to dry
  • Burning smell or very hot exterior
See all dryer repair problems

Oven and cooktop repair

Electric ovens, ranges and cooktops.

  • Oven won't heat or heats unevenly
  • Burner won't light or stay on
  • Broken or burnt element
  • Door won't latch or seal
See all oven and cooktop repair problems

Dishwasher repair

Water left behind, leaks, and dishes that come out dirty.

  • Dishes not coming out clean
  • Not draining
  • Leaking onto the floor
  • Won't start
See all dishwasher repair problems

Fridge repair

Fridges and freezers that are warm, frosted or leaking.

  • Not cooling properly
  • Freezer works, fridge is warm
  • Water under the unit
  • Loud or constant running
See all fridge repair problems

Appliance installation

New or replacement appliances connected and tested.

  • Washer and dryer hookups
  • Dishwasher installation
  • Oven and cooktop installation
  • Removal of the old unit
See all appliance installation problems
