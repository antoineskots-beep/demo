Frequently asked questions

What appliances do you install?

We install washers, dryers, dishwashers, ovens and cooktops, and remove the old unit when you need it gone.

Do you test the appliance after installing it?

Yes. We level the unit, check connections for leaks and run it before we leave.

What should I prepare before the visit?

Clear the path to the spot, make sure the water shut-off valves are reachable, and have the new appliance delivered and unpacked if possible.
