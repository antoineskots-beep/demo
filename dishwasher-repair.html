Frequently asked questions

Why is my dishwasher not draining?

A clogged filter, a blocked drain hose or a failed drain pump are the usual causes. Standing water at the bottom after a cycle is the main sign.

Why are my dishes still dirty after a cycle?

Blocked spray arms, a clogged filter, a faulty detergent dispenser or low water intake can all cause it.

Why is my dishwasher leaking?

A worn door gasket, a loose hose or a cracked tub fitting can leak. Stop using the machine until it is checked to avoid floor damage.
