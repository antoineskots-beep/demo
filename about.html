About Lafix

A local repair service for homes in Laval and Montreal.

Repairs, not replacements, when it makes sense

Lafix fixes washers, dryers, ovens, cooktops, dishwashers and fridges at your home, and installs new ones. We work on all major brands.

A technician finds the fault, tells you what it is and what it costs, and fixes it. If the part or the repair is not worth the money, we say so.

Our phone lines are open from 6am to 8pm, every day, because appliances do not break on a schedule.
