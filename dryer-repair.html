Frequently asked questions

Why is my dryer not heating?

The usual causes are a blown thermal fuse, a burnt heating element, a faulty thermostat or a power supply problem. A dryer that tumbles but stays cold is worth a call.

Why does my dryer take so long to dry?

A clogged lint filter or blocked vent is the first thing to check. If the vent is clear, a weak heating element or a failing thermostat is the likely cause.

Can a blocked dryer vent cause problems?

Yes. Restricted airflow makes the dryer run hot and slows drying, and built-up lint is a fire risk. We check the vent during the visit.
