Frequently asked questions

Why is my fridge not cooling properly?

Dirty condenser coils, a failed fan, a faulty thermostat or a compressor problem are the usual causes.

Why is my freezer working but my fridge section warm?

That usually points to a failed evaporator fan, a blocked air vent or a defrost problem. Call early, as food spoils quickly.

How long does food stay safe if my fridge stops working?

A closed fridge keeps food cold for about four hours. Keep the doors shut and call us as soon as you notice the problem.
