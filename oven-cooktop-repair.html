Frequently asked questions

Why is my oven not heating?

A burnt-out heating element, a faulty temperature sensor or a failed control board are the usual causes.

Why does my oven heat unevenly?

A weak element, a faulty sensor or a broken convection fan can leave hot and cold spots. Food that burns on one side is a common sign.
